Pilot brief / HR leadership / October 30, 2026

Talent Acquisition Agent pilot

Decision requested

Approve a 12-week pilot on three open requisitions, starting with the SW Factory Lead. The software is built. What stands between us and a pilot is access, approvals and one calibration run, not engineering.

The pilot runs alongside the current process, so no requisition depends on the tool while we measure it.

What the tool does

  • Scores candidates from licensed sources (LinkedIn Recruiter, ClearanceJobs and ATS exports, People Data Labs) against a rubric the hiring manager approves.
  • Quotes the profile behind every score and checks each quote automatically. A score it cannot support shows as unknown.
  • Hides name, contact details, location, schools and graduation years from the scoring model.
  • Logs every rubric approval, search, review and export.

What it does not do

  • Scrape LinkedIn, Indeed or any other site.
  • Contact anyone. Recruiters review the slate and send every message.
  • Make or recommend hiring decisions.
  • Verify clearances. Clearance is shown as self-reported until the security office confirms it after an offer.

Pilot plan

  1. Weeks 1 to 3Prerequisites from the list below: hosting, model access, data access, legal review, do-not-target list.
  2. Weeks 4 to 5Calibration on three to five closed requisitions, comparing the tool's ranking with the recruiters' actual picks.
  3. Weeks 6 to 12Live use on three open requisitions, in parallel with the current process, tuned weekly.
  4. Week 12Go, adjust or stop, judged against the targets below, agreed before the pilot starts.

What we need

  • IT security: hosting in Astrion's cloud behind single sign-on, and Claude access through Amazon Bedrock or an Anthropic API key.
  • Contracts: a do-not-target list from non-solicitation terms in teaming agreements, subcontracts and NDAs.
  • Employment counsel: review of state AI hiring rules (Illinois, California, New York City, Colorado from January 2027) and the data vendors' FCRA position.
  • Talent Acquisition: a senior recruiter as product owner, about a quarter of their time, and hiring managers to approve rubrics.

Risks and controls

  • Compensation: senior staff at Anduril, Shield AI and Applied Intuition often hold private equity. The tool flags it; the pilot measures response rates.
  • Legal: human review of every candidate, masked scoring, an audit trail and counsel sign-off before live use.
  • Model error: unsupported quotes are removed automatically. A fabricated quote reaching a recruiter stops the pilot until fixed.
  • Data: licensed sources only, with a 12-month retention purge for candidates nobody approved.
